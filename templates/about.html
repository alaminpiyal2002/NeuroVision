Project Overview

Brain tumors — especially gliomas — pose critical challenges in neuro-oncology. MRI-based diagnosis is time-consuming and prone to observer variability. This project introduces an automated deep-learning system designed to:

• analyze T1, T1C, T2, and FLAIR MRI sequences • detect tumor regions with high precision • evaluate tumor progression across multiple timepoints • forecast potential recurrence • assist clinicians with fast, quantitative insights

Through segmentation, temporal modeling, and 3D visualization, the system supports radiologists and aims to improve decision-making in clinical settings.
